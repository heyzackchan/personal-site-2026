## The brief

Northfield had outgrown its rubber stamp. The new café needed a brand that felt as honest as the coffee, and that could stretch from a 12oz bag to a storefront.

## The idea

The mark is a single mountain inside a circle. It reads as a peak, a cup seen from above, and a compass point. It is simple enough to burn into wood and still holds up at favicon size.

## The result

A small system of mark, wordmark, five colours and one serif. Every roast level gets its own colourway, so customers can spot their bag across the shelf.
