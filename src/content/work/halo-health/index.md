## The brief

Halo users felt buried in charts. The team wanted a site and web app that told people how they were doing in under three seconds.

## The idea

One ring. It fills as you hit your goals for the day. Everything else sits one tap away. The marketing site uses the same ring as its hero, so the product and the promise look identical.

## The result

A design system of 40 components, a new marketing site, and a web dashboard. Time on the onboarding page dropped while sign ups went up.
